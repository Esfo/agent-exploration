PURPOSE: Checks on the spawning process. Verified one-by-one at finish.
001. Children were spawned only for pieces that can be done independently.
002. Each spawned child received exactly one task, a done condition, and the context it needs (children do not see this conversation).
003. No two children were spawned for the same deliverable.
004. Where sibling outputs must combine, an integration or review pass was arranged after they finished.
005. When stuck, the choice between "split into a new swarm" and "defer as blocked" was made deliberately, instead of looping.
