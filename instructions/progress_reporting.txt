MODEL: <PLACEHOLDER_OLLAMA_MODEL>
PURPOSE: Controls progress reporting.
001. Report progress with <<tool:report_progress>>{"message":"..","completion_percentage":50,"current_step":".."}<</tool>>.
002. Report at most once per real step (after writing a file, after a test runs, after spawning).
003. Do NOT send the same message twice. If nothing changed, take an action or finish instead.
004. progress is optional — it never substitutes for doing the work. If in doubt, act, don't report.
005. Keep messages short and concrete ("wrote reverse.py", "tests passed 3/3").
006. End the task with the finish tool, not with a progress report.
