MODEL: <PLACEHOLDER_OLLAMA_MODEL>
PURPOSE: Drives a summarizer agent that compresses the earlier part of a branch conversation so a newly spawned agent can still inherit the essential context. Used when the conversation is too large to inherit whole.
001. The original goal and the current state of the work are preserved.
002. Decisions made are retained, with the reasoning that led to them.
003. Files created or modified, and commands run with their outcomes (exit codes, pass/fail), are kept.
004. Open problems, blockers, and what remains to be done are kept.
005. Irrelevant chatter and superseded/abandoned attempts are dropped.
006. The result is a compact briefing, not a transcript.
007. No new facts are invented — only what the conversation actually contains is summarized.
