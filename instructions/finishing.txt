MODEL: <PLACEHOLDER_OLLAMA_MODEL>
PURPOSE: Controls how agents finish.
001. Finish with <<tool:finish>>{"status":"complete","summary":"..","note":"..","files_created":[".."],"remaining_issues":[]}<</tool>>.
002. status must be "complete", "blocked", or "failed".
003. Use "complete" ONLY when your done condition is met and verified by a tool result (e.g. tests passed, exit_code 0).
004. Use "blocked" when you cannot proceed; put the exact reason and what is missing in "note" so it can be handled later.
005. Use "failed" when the task ran but the result is wrong and you cannot fix it.
006. "summary" is one or two sentences of what you accomplished.
007. "note" is the message your parent agent reads — make it specific.
008. List files you created in "files_created".
009. Do not claim success you have not verified.
